SITEGUARD

02

Construction is one of the most dangerous industries: more than a fifth of workplace fatalities happen on construction sites, and not wearing a hardhat or vest is the most common citation. Four problems make this hard to fix by hand. People take PPE off when unobserved. A safety officer cannot watch several video feeds continuously. Naive computer vision makes it worse by flooding officers with flickering false alarms. And workers move close to heavy machinery with nothing tracking that proximity. SiteGuard targets the first three directly and lays groundwork for the fourth.
