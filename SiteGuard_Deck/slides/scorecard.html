To evaluate the system as a whole rather than just the model, tools/safety_scorecard.py combines four sub-scores into a 0 to 100 reliability score. Detection reliability is weighted highest at 35 percent, at 85. Latency scores 90, with average inference of 48 milliseconds on the laptop CPU. Uptime, from camera heartbeat continuity, is 95. Duplicate suppression is 100, because the episode logic never produced a duplicate incident row. The weighted total is 91.25, grade A plus. Separately, tools/benchmark_system.py load-tests the server with two to four simulated cameras at 5 frames per second and reports latency, queue wait, throughput and dropped frames.
