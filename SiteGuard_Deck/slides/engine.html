This is the core of SiteGuard. A single frame saying no hardhat is not evidence: the worker may have turned their head or been briefly occluded. So for each tracked worker we keep a 10-second window of observations. An item is confirmed missing only when it is missing in at least 70 percent of those frames, and the violation clears only when that share drops to 30 percent or below. The gap between the two thresholds is hysteresis, the same idea as a thermostat, and it stops alerts flapping on and off. The chart is illustrative: the early bump never reaches the confirm line, so no alert; the real violation opens exactly one incident, which closes once the worker puts the hardhat back on. If a second item goes missing during the episode, the same incident is updated rather than duplicated.
