06

The camera node is an AI-Thinker ESP32-CAM: a dual-core 240 MHz microcontroller with an OV2640 2-megapixel sensor, 4 MB of PSRAM and built-in Wi-Fi, all for a few hundred rupees. It does no AI itself; it captures JPEG frames and ships them to a local PC that runs the heavy model. This edge-to-host split keeps the node cheap, cool and low-power. The table on the right is the standard AI-Thinker camera pinout we configure in firmware: the SCCB control bus on GPIO 26 and 27, clock and sync lines, and the 8-bit parallel data bus. Power is a stabilised 5 V supply.
