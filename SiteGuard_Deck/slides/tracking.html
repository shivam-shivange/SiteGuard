10

Our safety rules reason about a worker over ten seconds, so the worker must keep the same ID for that long. Pure motion trackers such as SORT or ByteTrack rely on a Kalman filter predicting where a box will move; they lose a worker who stops, walks behind a pillar, or crosses another worker. BoT-SORT adds two things: an appearance embedding, so a returning worker is recognised by how they look, and global motion compensation, which cancels camera vibration. The diagram shows worker 07 vanishing behind a pillar and being re-identified as 07, not given a new ID. These are the exact values in our botsort_reid.yaml. ByteTrack remains available as a lighter option.
