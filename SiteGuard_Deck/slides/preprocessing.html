SITEGUARD

08

Construction sites are hard lighting: harsh shadows under scaffolding, backlit workers against the sky, dawn and dusk. A plain brightness boost washes out colour, and colour is exactly what identifies a hardhat or a vest. So we convert to the CIE LAB colour space, which separates lightness from colour, and run CLAHE, contrast limited adaptive histogram equalization, only on the lightness channel. It boosts contrast tile by tile, and the clip limit of 2.0 stops it amplifying noise. Colour channels are untouched, so safety yellow and orange stay true. The illustration is a simplified depiction of the effect, not a real frame. The whole step costs a few milliseconds.
