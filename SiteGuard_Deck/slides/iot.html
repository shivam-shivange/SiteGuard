Beyond the camera, a second ESP32 can carry cheap sensors and actuators that reuse the same server. The four highlighted are the best value for a capstone: a buzzer and LED tower for on-site alarms; gas and temperature sensors, which add environmental hazards and make the mask rule context-aware; an RFID gate that only unlocks once the camera confirms full PPE and ties incidents to named workers; and a small OLED status display. The pills are further options: presence radar to wake cameras, ultrasonic proximity near machinery, wearable fall detection, an SOS button, LoRa for large sites, solar-powered camera poles, and a Jetson to run the model on site.
