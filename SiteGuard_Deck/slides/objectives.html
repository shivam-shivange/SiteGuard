03 · Objectives

Five objectives shaped every design choice

01

Edge ingestion

Stream QVGA frames from wireless ESP32-CAM nodes at 5 FPS.

02

Accurate attribution

Tie each PPE item to the right worker by box containment.

03

Stable identity

Keep worker IDs through occlusions with BoT-SORT Re-ID.

04

Zero flapping

Alert only after PPE is missing in 70% of a 10 s window.

05

Live visibility

WebSocket alerts, snapshot evidence and SQLite logging.

SITEGUARD

03

These five objectives are the yardstick for the rest of the talk. One: get frames off cheap wireless camera nodes reliably. Two: decide which worker each hardhat or vest belongs to, even when people stand side by side. Three: keep a worker's identity when they walk behind a pillar or cross another worker. Four: never flood the dashboard with flickering alerts, so a violation must persist across 70 percent of a 10-second window. Five: make every confirmed violation visible immediately, with photo evidence and a permanent log.
