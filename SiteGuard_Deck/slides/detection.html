SITEGUARD

09

The detector is YOLOv8 nano, the smallest Ultralytics model at about 6 megabytes, trained on our 10 construction classes. It runs at 640 by 640 on a laptop CPU, and we support ONNX Runtime for extra CPU throughput. The key design choice is asymmetric confidence gates. Missing a worker is the worst failure, so Person has the lowest gate at 0.35. A false violation alarm erodes trust, so the NO-hardhat, NO-vest and NO-mask classes need 0.50. Worn PPE and cones sit at 0.40, machinery and vehicles at 0.45. We also tightened non-maximum suppression to 0.40 to remove duplicate overlapping boxes, especially vest boxes overlapping person boxes.
