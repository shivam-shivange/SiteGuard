04 · Features

Nine capabilities work as one pipeline

Video ingestion

Multi-camera JPEG over HTTP POST from ESP32-CAM or webcam.

PPE detection

Person, hardhat, vest, mask and their NO- violation states.

Tracking & Re-ID

BoT-SORT with appearance embeddings; ByteTrack as an option.

PPE-to-worker match

Box containment of 50% or more ties gear to its wearer.

Temporal hysteresis

10 s window, 70% to confirm, 30% to clear: no false flicker.

Incident logging

SQLite in WAL mode with severity and snapshot evidence.

Live WebSocket feed

Alerts and per-worker compliance pushed to the dashboard.

Health telemetry

Heartbeats, RSSI and free heap; offline after 15 s of silence.

Benchmarking

Multi-camera load tests and a 0–100 reliability score.

This is the feature map. Read it top-left to bottom-right and it follows the data: frames come in, workers and PPE are detected, workers are tracked with re-identification, gear is matched to its wearer, the hysteresis engine decides whether a violation is real, incidents are logged with evidence and pushed live to the dashboard. The bottom row is operational: device health telemetry, and the tooling we built to benchmark the system and score its reliability.
