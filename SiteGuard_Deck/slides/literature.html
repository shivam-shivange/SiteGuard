Every major design decision traces back to published work. The dark cards are implemented: BoT-SORT and ByteTrack for tracking, YOLOv8 for detection, CLAHE for preprocessing, Fang's containment approach for PPE attribution, hysteresis voting from anomaly detection, the edge-cloud split, Dean and Barroso's tail-latency ideas behind our drop-oldest frame queue, write-ahead logging in SQLite, ONNX Runtime, and OSNet-style Re-ID embeddings. Ray and Teizer's struck-by proximity work is partial: we detect machinery and vehicles but don't yet compute real-world distances. TinyML on the node, pose estimation and IoT actuation are planned, and they lead into the roadmap.
